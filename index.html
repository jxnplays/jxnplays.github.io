REQUIREMENTS

BRING YOUR OWN GAMES.

A legally owned Steam install. The client reads that folder. It does not download or replace the game. Modern Warfare 2 is required. The others are optional. Call of Duty 3 joins from its own install.

UNDER THE GAMES

THE MATCH TYPES.

A mode is listed only if that game shipped it, except Third Person. That is the SOCOM-style camera. Modern Warfare 2 shipped it. Hardcore and Old School are rules, not modes.
