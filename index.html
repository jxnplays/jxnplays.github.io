UNDER THE GAMES

THE MATCH TYPES.

A mode is listed only if that game shipped it, except Third Person. That is the SOCOM-style camera. Modern Warfare 2 shipped it. The aim is the same camera on the other match types. Hardcore and Old School are rules, not modes.

AFTER THE RETAIL LINE

CUSTOM MAPS.

IW4x, Plutonium, and the usermaps folders. They stay in their own list. They do not get filed as Modern Warfare 2, Modern Warfare 3, or Black Ops.

IW4X

User maps and the DLC-style packs that live in that client. Labeled custom.

PLUTONIUM

Its maps, guns, and camos, kept in that folder. Labeled custom.

USERMAPS

A map you dropped in. It shows as a custom map, with its own image if the pack has one.
