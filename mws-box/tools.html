Tools

Custom tools built for this project. Some target games already have usable community readers or converters available elsewhere; those are not listed here.

NX1 Fastfile Reader

Project-specific reader for Future Warfare (NX1) fastfiles. Header and marker work documented. In progress.

Coming soon

NX1 Asset Converter

Converts extracted NX1 viewmodels, hands, and animations into formats usable by the IW4L client. Bone count differences (NX1 has 59 vs IW4's 34) are the main challenge.

Per-game Asset Readers & Converters

Additional readers and converters for titles where existing community tools are incomplete or missing the pieces we need for the client. Open for contributors.
