Future Warfare (NX1)

~2011–2013 (canceled) — Neversoft

In progress

NX1 is treated as a namespace and DLC category inside the IW4L client, the same way other titles sit behind their own family IDs. It does not fork the main render path. One weapon (SCAR Mod 2) is currently playable with donor textures; the reusable reader and converter work is further along.

Current state

One playable weapon is roughly 70% complete. The reusable reader and converter engine is roughly 90% complete. The main open problem is texture binding: materials read and load correctly, but applying them in first-person view still requires further work. Sounds and maps have not been started. The format and reader work is the largest completed piece so far.
