FEATURES

THE SANDBOX.

A Rust and Bevy client. It reads the games you own. It does not ship them. The graphics board is aimed at Modern Warfare 2 Remastered parity. Anti-aliasing, filtering, water, weather, and light are planned. None of that is in a match yet.

YOUR FILES

Point it at the Steam folder. It reads the fastfiles there. It does not download or replace them.

ONE LOBBY

Fifteen games in one client. Modern Warfare 2 is the base. The rest add only what that install contains.

CREATE A CLASS

Guns, equipment, perks, and deathstreaks, split by game, plus an All list. Modern Warfare 2, 3, and Black Ops are the first three.

MATCH TYPES

Only the modes that game shipped. Third Person is the SOCOM-style camera. Hardcore and Old School are rules.

AMBIENT OCCLUSION PLANNED

Contact shadow in corners and under cover. A video setting, not a baked look.

WATER PLANNED

A new water shader for maps that have it. Reflection and motion, not the flat retail sheet.

WEATHER PLANNED

Rain, fog, and clear. The options menu can list them. The world effect is still to be wired.

LIGHT AND SHADOW PLANNED

Sun direction, shadow quality, and a night pass. Per map, not one global grade.

ANTI-ALIASING PLANNED

FXAA, SMAA, and MSAA. A modern edge pass, not the retail jaggies.

FILTERING PLANNED

Anisotropic filtering and texture quality. Ground and walls stay sharp at an angle.

ROADMAP

WHAT IS IN A MATCH.

Status from playtests, not from the install. In game means it loaded and rendered. Wiring means the asset is known and the menu path is not finished. Not in source means the table or image list is not in the readable files. Needs a reader means that game cannot be opened yet.
